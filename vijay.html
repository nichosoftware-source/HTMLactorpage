VIJAY

Joseph Vijay Chandrasekhar (born 22 June 1974), known mononymously as Vijay, is an Indian politician and former actor who transitioned to politics. You can read his full biography on the C. Joseph Vijay Wikipedia Page. He is the founder and president of the political party Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK) and has served as the Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu.
